# 🐚 Sea Urchin Catcher

A mobile-friendly arcade game where you control a diver to catch falling sea urchins in the beautiful Tasmanian ocean!

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Move the diver** with your mouse or touch to catch falling sea urchins
- **Score 10 points** for each urchin caught
- **Giant kelp grows** as you catch more urchins (every 30 points)
- **3 lives** represented by red handfish ❤
- **Game over** when you miss 3 urchins
- **Difficulty increases** as your score goes up

## 🌊 Features

- Tasmanian ocean-themed background with deep blue waters and reef
- Progressive difficulty with faster falling urchins
- Kelp forest growth system
- Mobile-responsive design - play on any device
- Touch and mouse controls

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Clone this repository
2. Open `index.html` in your browser
3. Play and enjoy!

## 📱 QR Code Access

Generate a QR code pointing to: `https://your-github-pages-url/sea-urchin-catcher`

Users can scan the QR code with their phone camera to play instantly!
